Admission Protocol

  • Allocating a bed instantly reserves the slot and marks it Occupied across the hospital system.
  • Once admitted, an Advance Deposit can be collected immediately with UPI/Card UTR tracking.
  • Daily bed charges will accrue based on the ward's published tariff.
  • Discharge requires attending doctor authorization and clinical summary completion.

Vedant Inpatient Standards

All inpatients must receive admission wristband identification and digital intake record verification prior to ward transfer.
